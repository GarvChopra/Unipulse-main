UNIFIX — Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your UNIFIX account and associated personal data. Operated by {{ glb.name }}.

Delete from inside the app

Sign in, open Profile (tap your initial, top-right) and choose Delete my account. This immediately:

  • signs you out and disables your login,
  • flags your account for deletion and notifies the campus administrator,
  • records the request in the audit log.

Request without signing in

Email {{ contact }} from your institute address with the subject "UNIFIX account deletion". The administrator will verify and process it.

What is deleted

DeletedRetained
Your name, username, department, contact email and PIN hash. Infrastructure issue reports and their resolution history are kept as institutional maintenance records, but are de-identified — your name is removed and replaced with "Former staff". Photos you attached to a report remain part of that maintenance record.

De-identification rather than full erasure of the reports is necessary because the records document the condition and repair history of campus infrastructure. If you need the reports themselves removed, state that in your request and the institute will assess it.

Timeframe

Account access is revoked immediately on request. De-identification is completed by the administrator, normally within 30 days.
